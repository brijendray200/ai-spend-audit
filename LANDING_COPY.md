# Landing Copy

- Hero headline: Find wasted AI spend fast
- Subheadline: Audit your AI stack, right-size plans, and uncover savings before the next renewal hits.
- Primary CTA copy: Run the free audit
- Social proof block: Mocked examples from seed-stage startups that cut redundant AI subscriptions and bought credits more efficiently.
- FAQ 1: Do I need to connect billing data?
- FAQ 1 answer: No. The MVP works from self-reported tools, plans, seats, and spend so founders can get value immediately.
- FAQ 2: Why does the tool ask for email after the result?
- FAQ 2 answer: Because the product is designed to prove value first and capture the lead only after the report is visible.
- FAQ 3: Is the recommendation just “switch tools” every time?
- FAQ 3 answer: No. The engine prefers same-vendor downgrades and pricing corrections before suggesting a workflow change.
- FAQ 4: What happens if my stack is already efficient?
- FAQ 4 answer: The app says so clearly and still offers a notification flow for future optimization opportunities.
- FAQ 5: What makes this useful for Credex?
- FAQ 5 answer: High-savings reports naturally create a qualified path into discounted AI credits and a consultation.
